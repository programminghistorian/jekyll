Application to participate: ENABLAR

ENABLAR (ENABling Library and Archive participation in digital Research co-learning communities) is a new Jisc-funded project from Programming Historian that will encourage participation and facilitate co-production to empower co-learning communities. If you'd like to participate, please complete this form. 

Our blogpost provides more information about the project <https://tinyurl.com/enablar-blog>
Please check the indicative dates of the orientation sessions in December 2025.
We'll be selecting a cohort of ~10 people, balancing representation across library and archives professionals and digital humanities researchers. We'll write to let you know if your application is successful.
